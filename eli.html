My Baby, Eli

My baby,

I don't think I'll ever find enough words to explain just how much I love you. Somehow, you came into my life and brought so much joy into it — a kind of joy I didn't even know I was missing.

I love you not because you have to be someone else or because you have to be perfect. I love you for being you. For the way you laugh, the way you care, the little things you do, and even the parts of yourself you sometimes don't realize are worth loving.

You deserve to be loved in every special way possible. You deserve a love that makes you feel safe, appreciated, chosen, and understood. I hope I can always remind you that you are worthy of that kind of love.

Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for all the happiness you've brought into my days and for making ordinary moments feel a little more special. Having you in my life is something I'll always be grateful for.

I love you so, so much, Eli. More than I can probably put into a website, a letter, or even words.

And if I had to choose you all over again, I'd still choose you. Always.

I love you ♡
